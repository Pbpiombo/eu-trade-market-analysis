# Project Questions

### Trade openness and employment: 
Do EU countries with greater trade openness (total trade — exports plus imports — as a share of GDP) show different unemployment levels?

### Trade composition and employment: 
Is the weight of manufactured goods in a country's trade associated with different unemployment levels?
### Reaction to economic shocks: 
How did trade and unemployment react to the 2009 and 2020 crises, and which countries recovered fastest?
